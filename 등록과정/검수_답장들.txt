2. App Purpose & Target Audience:
- Purpose: "OneDay Trip" is a location-based travel itinerary and local recommendation platform designed to help travelers easily discover day-trip courses, tourist attractions, and local restaurants based on their real-time GPS location.
- Target Audience: Domestic and international travelers looking for spontaneous day trips, weekend outings, and authentic local travel experiences in South Korea. The app supports 4 languages (Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese) to accommodate international tourists.

4. External Services, Tools, and Platforms Used:
- Cloud Hosting & Backend: Vercel (Web infrastructure)
- Location Hardware: Apple CoreLocation framework (GPS) & WebKit Geolocation
- Map & Navigation: Native iOS URL schemes for external map apps (Naver Map, KakaoMap, Tmap)
- Content & Data Sources: Curated travel datasets and Korea Tourism Organization (TourAPI) public open data.
- In-App Purchases / Payments: None (100% Free service).

5. Regional Differences:
The app operates consistently across all regions without geographic lock or regional feature restrictions. While the curated travel content is currently focused on destinations in South Korea, users anywhere in the world can browse itineraries and use the app identically.
